<p class="sc-lede">I lead climate and nature risk modelling, turning physical science into the financial terms that boards, investors and governments act on. I am also a hands-on engineer: I build the models and the data pipelines behind them, then take the results to the board.</p>

<p>I'm Canadian, and my career began in the field in British Columbia, flying LiDAR drones and installing weather stations. A full scholarship brought me to the University of Cambridge, and Bloomberg kept me in London. On secondment to GFANZ, I designed the data model behind a global climate data platform launched at COP28, and wrote speech content and figures delivered by Mark Carney, now Prime Minister of Canada, and Michael Bloomberg. Since then I have advised FTSE 100 boards on climate risk and now lead risk modelling at Risilience.</p>
